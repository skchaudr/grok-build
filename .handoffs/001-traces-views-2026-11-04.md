# Traces views: six redundant sessions (2026-11-04)

Sab asked twice for three *distinct* follow + sessions views. Both rounds produced one design.

**Round 1, three Opus agents** (branches `traces/proto-a|b|c`). Claude's brief fixed the look (grok's Dashboard), the five views, the density toggle and the summaries, and gave the follow view one line. All three built the same sessions list (about 1,000 to 1,300 lines of Rust each) and left the follow view as grok's own session view. Small differences: B groups by day and shows active time; C shows running subagents under their parent with their latest content. **C is merged, pushed (code only) and installed on the Mini and sab-air** (main `1d7a903d`).

**Round 2, three Grok 4.7 agents** (`traces/grok-a|b|c`), plus `traces/grok-live`. Claude pasted Sab's coordinator goal to each verbatim and started them from the three alike prototypes, so none had a distinct job.
- grok-a: a rail of every agent in the session beside the live tail.
- grok-b: Enter opens every harness (OMP, bb, Cursor, DSH) in grok's view.
- grok-c: follow deck, one agent's stream with an "Also live" strip of the others.
- grok-live: live calls from receipts/spans merged into one row per call (compile fix committed).

Builds: `~/traces-checkpoints/grok-{a,b,c}/`. Screenshots of a and c: sab-air `~/docs/traces-salvage/`. All seven branches are also in `~/traces-checkpoints/traces-branches-2026-11-04.bundle`. Worktrees are removed.

**The variety already existed** in trace-deck's `PLAN.md`, from Sab's own vision, and no agent was shown it:
- Dispatch tree: sessions parent to child; selecting a child focuses its transcript.
- Timeline: each agent's tool calls as bars on one shared time axis.
- Wall: every live session as a tile with what it's doing now, a context bar and an events-per-minute sparkline.
- Context pressure: a gauge per session, changing colour past 70% and 90%.
- Rich rows: edits as diffs, commands as code, text as markdown, images inline.
- Search with a minimap of hits on the scrollbar.

trace-deck's `LIVE-GUI-SPEC.md` adds the later stages: driving sessions through Herdr and Rex.

**Build note:** worktrees under `.claude/worktrees/` make cargo read two configs and double the rustflags, so a shared build folder rebuilds everything; and cargo's timestamp check reuses another copy's crates. Pass `RUSTFLAGS` once and `touch` the copy's sources before building.
